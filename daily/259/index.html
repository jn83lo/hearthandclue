About The Daily Clue

A free word search, new every day. Each puzzle is a 10×10 grid hiding eight words on one theme, and everyone gets the same puzzle that day. The next one appears at midnight, wherever you are.

Missed a day? Play the past puzzles.

Words run in straight lines: across, down or diagonally, forwards or backwards. Drag across a word to mark it, or use Reveal a word if you’re stuck. When you finish, you can share your result.

Themes rotate through camping, golf, quilting, gardening, fishing, cozy mysteries and more, with specials for Halloween, Christmas and Valentine’s Day. No login, no ads, nothing to install.

New: free printable word searches in large print, with answer keys: Halloween and fall.
