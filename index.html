    A free word search, every day

    The Daily Clue is a large print word search puzzle that changes every day. Everyone solves the same grid on the same day, so you can compare your time with a friend. It's free, there's nothing to install, and there's no sign-up.

    Each day draws on a different cosy theme — the garden, Sunday roast, the bird feeder, the workshop — with eight words hidden in the grid. If you get stuck, reveal one and carry on.

    The puzzles come from Hearth & Clue, where you'll find 30 printable large print puzzle books as instant PDF downloads — themed word searches, sudoku, and story-driven cosy mysteries. Solutions always included.
