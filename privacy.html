Privacy

Last updated 5 September 2026

The short version. The puzzle needs nothing from you. There is no account, no sign-up and no newsletter. We do not set cookies, run advertising, or load analytics or tracking scripts. We do not sell or share anything, because we do not collect anything to sell.

What the puzzle stores

The Daily Clue runs entirely in your browser. The day’s grid is worked out from the date, so everyone gets the same puzzle without us needing to know who you are. Your progress through a puzzle stays in the browser tab and is gone when you close it.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, their servers keep standard request logs, which can include an IP address and browser type. We do not use those logs to identify anyone. See Netlify’s privacy policy.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google to fetch them. See Google’s privacy policy.

Links away from here

Some pages link to our Etsy shop or to Pinterest. Once you follow a link you are on their site under their rules, not ours.

Our Pinterest posting

We publish the day’s puzzle image to our own Pinterest board automatically. It posts only artwork we made ourselves, to one board we own. It does not read, collect or publish anything belonging to Pinterest users.

Children

The puzzle is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about any of this can go through the Hearth & Clue Etsy shop, where you can message us directly.
